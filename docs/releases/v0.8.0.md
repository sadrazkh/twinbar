## 🕒 Twinbar by the clock

**Your profiles, one click away, all the time.** Twinbar now lives in the
notification area: right-click its icon and open any profile, see which ones
are running and whether each one's proxy is up. It can start with Windows, and
your profiles are in its jump list too.

### ✨ New

- 🖱️ **An icon by the clock.** Click it for the window. Right-click it for your
  profiles, each with its icon and a note: *running*, *proxy OK*, *proxy down*.
  One click opens one. The same menu has the update (when there is one),
  Twinbar's switches, and **Quit Twinbar**.
- 🙈 **Closing the window keeps Twinbar there**, and it says so the first
  time. Don't like that? Untick **Keep Twinbar by the clock when its window
  closes**.
- 🚀 **Start with Windows** (off until you tick it): just the icon by the
  clock, ready when you are. Uninstalling removes it.
- 📋 **A jump list.** Right-click Twinbar's taskbar button, or its pinned icon,
  and your profiles are right there.
- ⚙️ **`twinbar settings`** for the switches: `updates`, `tray`, `startup`.

```powershell
twinbar settings startup on
twinbar settings
```

- 🔁 Opening Twinbar while it runs by the clock simply shows its window, and
  `Twinbar.exe --quit` ends it.
- 📝 The window keeps a short log in `%APPDATA%\Twinbar\logs\window.log`, handy
  when you report a problem.

### 🧪 Tested end to end

Starting by the clock, showing the window from a second `Twinbar.exe`,
hiding it with the X button, and quitting, all run against the real
`Twinbar.exe`, here and on GitHub's Windows runners. (The window log also
caught a race in the tests on GitHub's runner; it's fixed.)
